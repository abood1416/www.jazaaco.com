Concrete Cooling Solutions

Watch Our Cooling Solutions in Action

Established in 1996, our partner is a leading organization engaged in design, manufacturing, exporting, and supplying a wide range of products like Flake Ice Plant, Tube Ice Plant, and Chilled Water Plant.

Cooling Technologies

  • Flake Ice Plants: Production capacity from 5 to 100 tons per day, delivering ice at -7°C for rapid concrete temperature reduction.
  • Chilled Water Systems: Closed-loop cooling with plate heat exchangers to maintain precise concrete mixing temperatures.
  • Tube Ice Plant: Produces clear, cylindrical ice with a hole in the middle by freezing water inside vertical stainless steel tubes.
  • Automatic Ice Handling: A mechanized system of screw conveyors, belts, and pneumatic blowers moving ice without manual labor.
