Nahdi Pharmacy

Complete renovation of the pharmacy exterior.

Department: Architecture & Construction / Production House

Client: Nahdi

Status: Completed

Scope:

  • Branch Renovations: Full and partial renovation works across numerous branches (including Awaser, Manar, Judi, Basala, Dafiaha, Suhail, Almira, Firdouse, and the Nahdi and Saba clinic locations), plus a first-floor renovation and dedicated female toilet renovations at select sites.
  • Electrical & Lighting: Electrical panel replacement, installation of electrical sockets, floor lighting upgrades across a 3-branch cluster, and general light fittings.
  • Safety & Fire Protection: Full replacement of a fire-fighting system and installation/repair of exit doors across multiple branches.
  • Plumbing & Utilities: Water tank replacement at multiple branches, a diesel tank supply and installation, and a bad-odor/plumbing diagnostic investigation at the Saba Clinic.
  • Structural & Finishing Works: ACP cladding installation and reinstallation, handrail repairs, interlocks repair and maintenance, damaged glass replacement, and window curtain installation.
  • Retail Fit-Out: Supply and installation of shelving and racking units (including a 6-unit shelving and racking package and warehouse-wide shelf supply), a kitchen cabinet installation, and a complimentary mesh wall installation.
  • General Maintenance: Recurring maintenance visits across branches including Safa, Yamama, Obhur, and Ishaa.

    • How it started :

    It didn't start as a program — it started as a single exit door that needed fixing at one Nahdi branch in Jeddah. That first small job is easy to overlook in hindsight, but it's the one that mattered most: it put Jazaaco's name on file with one of Saudi Arabia's largest pharmacy retail networks, and it gave the team a chance to show up, do the work properly, and leave the branch running exactly as it should. The next call came for a maintenance visit. Then an electrical panel. Then a full branch renovation. Before long, the calls weren't for one branch anymore — they were coming in from across the city, for problems that had nothing in common except that Nahdi trusted the same team to solve them.

    By the time the program had run its course, Jazaaco had been inside more than 40 different branches and clinic sites — Safa, Rawdah, Obhur, Almira, the Wellness branch, the warehouse, and dozens more — each with its own version of the same underlying challenge: keep a live retail pharmacy open, safe, and presentable while the work happens around it. Some visits were quick — a water tank swap, a set of handrails, a light fitting. Others were far bigger: a diesel tank installed from scratch, an entire fire-fighting system replaced branch-wide, ACP cladding stripped and reinstalled on a facade that had seen better days. No two work orders looked alike, and that was the point — Nahdi wasn't hiring Jazaaco for one skill, it was leaning on Jazaaco as the team that could be handed almost anything and be trusted to bring the right trade to the job.

    Execution:

    Because every branch on the list was a live, operating pharmacy — and in some cases a clinic seeing patients — nothing could be planned the way it would be on an empty construction site. Renovations and cladding work were scheduled around store hours; electrical panel changeovers were timed for the quietest part of the day; maintenance and repair calls were run as same-day, in-and-out visits so a branch was never closed longer than it had to be. Nahdi dealt with one point of contact regardless of which branch called or what the problem was, which meant new work orders could be picked up fast, without re-explaining the account to a new contractor every time.

    Results:

    By the numbers, this became 47 completed work orders across more than 30 branches and clinic locations — but the number that matters more is the one behind it: Nahdi kept calling. For a young company, that's the real outcome of this program. It wasn't won on a single big contract; it was earned one exit door, one water tank, one renovation at a time, until being on-call for Nahdi's Jeddah branches was simply how things worked. That kind of repeat trust from a national retail name is exactly the reference Jazaaco now leans on when pitching for other multi-site facilities accounts.

    Saudi Cable — Industrial Facility Maintenance & Upgrades

    Ongoing facility maintenance and upgrade works at an active industrial manufacturing site.

    Department: Industrial Supplies

    Client: Saudi Cable Company

    Location: Industrial Area, Jeddah

    Status: Completed

    Scope:

  • General Facility Maintenance: Ongoing maintenance works across the Industrial Area site.
  • Fuel Infrastructure: Supply and installation of a diesel tank at the Industrial Area-01 facility.
  • Protective & Finishing Works: Painting works across facility structures.
  • Security & Access: Supply and installation of a steel door at the Industrial Area-01 facility.

    • How it started :

    Walking onto Saudi Cable's Industrial Area site was a different kind of job for a team that had spent most of its time inside pharmacy branches and retail spaces. There's no foot traffic to work around on an industrial floor, but there are forklifts, production lines, and a facility that can't afford unplanned downtime — a different set of things to be careful about. The first ask was straightforward general maintenance. From there, the scope grew into the kind of work that only comes once a client decides they can hand you something with real consequence if it goes wrong: a diesel tank, supplied and installed at Industrial Area-01, sitting close enough to live operations that there was no room for a careless install.

    Alongside it came the less glamorous but equally necessary work — painting to keep facility structures protected, and a steel door installed for site access and security. None of it was headline construction, but all of it mattered to a facility that runs every day of the year.

    Execution:

    Every visit was coordinated directly with Saudi Cable's operations team first — what could run during production hours, what needed a shutdown window, and where the diesel tank installation in particular needed extra care given its proximity to active manufacturing. Nothing was scheduled on Jazaaco's clock alone; it moved around the plant's.

    Results:

    This is a small file in terms of line items, but a big one in terms of what it opened up: it's the account that moved Jazaaco from "renovates pharmacy branches" to "can be trusted on an active industrial floor." For a startup building out an Industrial Supplies division, that shift matters more than the size of any single job on the list — it's the credibility that gets Jazaaco a seat at the table for bigger industrial maintenance contracts going forward.

    Location: Jubail, Yanbu & Duba, and Riyadh (Diriyah)

    The result here isn't any single trench dug or fence installed — it's that all three happened at once, without Jazaaco having to say no to one of them. The Jubail work in particular put Jazaaco's manpower supply capability in front of the Aramco supply chain, even indirectly through SSG Group, which is exactly the kind of reference a startup wants when it's trying to build a name in oil & gas and industrial infrastructure. It's a small set of jobs individually, but together they tell a story about a company willing to go wherever the work is.
